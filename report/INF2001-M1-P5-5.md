## 1.4 Executive Summary

Operational analysis of the client music academy revealed substantial friction stemming from its reliance on shared spreadsheets: an estimated 8 to 12 hours wasted weekly on manual timetable construction (as reported by operational management in interview MIN-01), recurrent studio double-bookings, unmonitored fatigue risks (teachers assigned >4 hours of continuous instruction without rest intervals), over-allocation beyond internal workload guidelines (>40 hours/week), and communication delays during teacher absences.

To resolve these operational deficits, Team P5-5 engineered a web-based decision-support system featuring real-time manpower intelligence, side-by-side 3-teacher allocation matrices displaying physical location and availability, Studio 5 drum kit exclusivity guards, and labor protection monitoring in accordance with Singapore's Employment Act 1968 (Republic of Singapore, 1968). Consistent with modern work-life balance frameworks (Guest, 2002; Lupu & Ruiz-Castro, 2021), sustainable workload distribution is structured as an ongoing governance process. This Milestone 1 report formalizes the software requirements, behavioral models, and object-oriented analysis architecture establishing the foundation for system development.

# 3. Use Cases & Activity Diagrams

## 3.1 Actor Identification & System Scope

The system boundary encompasses all workload planning, clash validation, availability tracking, reporting, and administrative governance. The system interfaces with three primary human actors:
- **Manager:** Allocates weekly lessons, reviews workload metrics, resolves late availability and rejection exceptions, handles emergency absence coverage, and publishes confirmed timetables.
- **Teacher:** Submits recurring monthly availability, updates schedule preferences, inspects personal confirmed timetables, initiates bilateral lesson swaps, and submits job rejection tickets.
- **IT Administrator:** Provisions user accounts, configures physical studio parameters, and schedules maintenance blackout windows.

*UML Stereotype Relationships:*

- **`<<include>>` (Mandatory Sub-behaviors):** The factored-out use case `Validate Allocation Constraints` is unconditionally executed whenever the base use case allocates or re-allocates a lesson, ensuring that every placement—whether a new assignment, a substitute re-assignment, or a peer swap—is validated against the same multi-constraint clash and fatigue rules. In accordance with UML standards, the dependency arrow points strictly from the base use case to the included use case (`Base ..> Included`).
  - `UC-02 (Allocate Weekly Teaching Job)` `<<include>>` `Validate Allocation Constraints`
  - `UC-03 (Review & Re-allocate Rejected Lesson)` `<<include>>` `Validate Allocation Constraints`
  - `UC-06 (Handle Emergency Absence Substitute)` `<<include>>` `Validate Allocation Constraints`
  - `UC-11 (Request Peer Lesson Swap)` `<<include>>` `Validate Allocation Constraints`

- **`<<extend>>` (Conditional Extension Points):** Optional behaviour is modelled as extensions that trigger only when a specific condition holds. In accordance with UML standards, the dependency arrow points from the extending use case back to the base use case (`Extending ..> Base`).
  - `Authorize Overtime` `<<extend>>` `UC-02 (Allocate Weekly Teaching Job)` — triggers when weekly hours enter the advisory tier (40.5h–44.0h), prompting the manager for a soft-warning override.
  - `Authorize Overtime` `<<extend>>` `UC-06 (Handle Emergency Absence Substitute)` — triggers when assigning a qualified substitute pushes their weekly workload into the 40.5h–44.0h advisory range.

*Non-Actor Boundary Decisions:* In accordance with standard UML semantics, `School Management` is an external executive stakeholder receiving exported PDF reports out-of-band and is not a direct system actor. `Student` information is encapsulated as attributes within the `Lesson` entity; students do not directly log in or interact with the M1 system. Internal databases and notification services reside within the system boundary.

## 3.3 Formal Use Case Specifications

### 3.3.1 Manager Operational Use Cases

#### UC-01: View Workload Dashboard & Outliers
- **Primary Actor:** Manager
- **Trigger:** Manager accesses system home landing page.
- **Priority:** High | **Secondary Actors:** None
- **Brief Description:** Manager monitors teacher workload distribution, identifying under-allocated teachers and teachers approaching or exceeding weekly thresholds.
- **Preconditions:** Manager is authenticated with active credentials.
- **Postconditions:**
  - *Main Success:* Real-time dashboard renders progress bars, top 3 lowest-workload teachers, and color-coded workload alert tiers.
  - *Alternative Failure:* System displays cached metrics with a synchronization warning if live data is temporarily unreachable.
- **Main Success Scenario:**
  1. System queries current weekly schedule allocations across all active instructors.
  2. System calculates running weekly and monthly hours for each teacher.
  3. System renders workload dashboard featuring:
     - Sorted list of the top 3 teachers with the lowest monthly allocated hours.
     - 3-tier visual badges: Green (<35.0h), Amber (35.0h–40.0h), and Red (>40.0h).
  4. Manager reviews metrics to plan upcoming allocations.
- **Alternative Scenarios:**
  - *3a. Teacher approaches 44.0h statutory ceiling:* System highlights teacher row with a Red statutory capacity badge (44.0h reached) and displays an advisory notice indicating that the teacher is at the statutory maximum and ineligible for additional lesson allocations.

#### UC-02: Allocate Weekly Teaching Job
- **Primary Actor:** Manager
- **Trigger:** Manager selects an unassigned or confirmed lesson slot to allocate.
- **Priority:** High | **Secondary Actors:** None
- **Brief Description:** Manager assigns a 30-minute lesson to an instructor and studio for a specific week, assisted by side-by-side comparison and instant constraint validation.
- **Preconditions:**
  - Target calendar week exists in `Draft` state; lesson slot is selected.
  - *Static & dynamic candidate invariants (enforced upfront by candidate and studio filtering, never offered as invalid options):* only teachers holding active certification for the lesson instrument whose projected weekly hours <= 44.0h are eligible candidates; Drums lessons can only be placed in Studio 5, as it is the sole studio with an acoustic drum kit.
- **Postconditions:**
  - *Main Success:* Lesson is bound to teacher and studio with status `Confirmed`, workload tallies increment by 0.5h, and transaction is logged.
  - *Alternative Failure:* Assignment is blocked; system displays specific constraint violation feedback.
- **Main Success Scenario:**
  1. Manager selects target week and student lesson slot.
  2. System retrieves lesson parameters (Instrument, Student Name, Timeslot, Duration = 30 min).
  3. System filters the candidate pool to teachers who are certified for the lesson instrument, marked available for the timeslot in their submitted availability, within the 4.0-hour continuous teaching limit (mandatory 1.0-hour rest break) if assigned, and whose projected weekly workload (current workload + 0.5h) <= 44.0h. System renders a candidate comparison panel displaying up to 3 of these teachers side-by-side with running weekly hours, monthly totals, preferences, and physical studio location for that date.
  4. Manager selects a candidate teacher. System restricts the studio picker to studios that are compatible with the lesson instrument (Studio 5 only for Drums), unoccupied, and not under a maintenance blackout window for the timeslot; all other studios are disabled. Manager selects an eligible studio.
  5. System re-validates the selected teacher, studio, and timeslot against the current schedule before saving.
  6. System evaluates projected teacher weekly hours (current workload + 0.5h):
     - If total hours <= 40.0h, assignment proceeds nominally.
     - If total hours > 40.0h and <= 44.0h (Advisory Tier), system prompts manager for soft-warning override and logs justification.
  7. System assigns lesson to teacher and studio, setting status to `Confirmed`.
  8. System increments teacher weekly and monthly workload tallies by 0.5h.
  9. System persists assignment and records immutable audit log entry.
  10. System renders updated schedule grid reflecting new booking.
- **Alternative Scenarios:**
  - *5a. Concurrent allocation conflict:* Another manager has booked the selected teacher or studio for the same timeslot after the options were displayed. System blocks placement displaying: "Allocation Conflict: Selection no longer available." and returns to step 3 with refreshed candidates and studios.

#### UC-03: Review & Re-allocate Rejected Lesson
- **Primary Actor:** Manager
- **Trigger:** System receives a teacher rejection ticket and flags the exception queue.
- **Priority:** High | **Secondary Actors:** Student, Substitute Teacher
- **Brief Description:** Manager reviews an instructor-rejected lesson, contacts the teacher if needed, and either rebinds the lesson to a qualified substitute or releases the room and queues the student for priority rescheduling.
- **Preconditions:** Target lesson is in `Pending Re-allocation` state following teacher rejection.
- **Postconditions:**
  - *Main Success:* Lesson reassigned to qualified substitute teacher with status `Confirmed`, or released with status `Unassigned` and student priority queued.
  - *Alternative Failure:* Lesson remains in `Pending Re-allocation`; alert persists on dashboard.
- **Main Success Scenario:**
  1. Manager accesses the Exception Queue on the dashboard.
  2. System displays pending rejections with teacher ID, student, instrument, slot, reason code, and remarks.
  3. Manager reviews rejection ticket and contacts teacher to explore resolution.
  4. Manager elects to reallocate lesson and queries available qualified substitutes.
  5. System executes multi-tier candidate pre-filtering:
     - *Static Pre-condition:* Filters instructors holding active certification for the lesson instrument.
     - *Dynamic Pre-condition:* Filters instructors who are available during the 30-minute slot, whose continuous teaching block does not exceed 4.0h, and whose projected weekly workload <= 44.0h.
     - System presents candidate substitute teachers meeting all criteria.
  6. Manager selects substitute teacher.
  7. If substitute's projected hours fall in the 40.5h–44.0h advisory range, manager confirms soft-warning override. System rebinds lesson to substitute, decrements original teacher hours by 0.5h, increments substitute hours by 0.5h, and transitions status to `Confirmed`.
  8. System dispatches confirmation notices to student and substitute teacher.
  9. System appends resolution outcome to immutable audit trail.
- **Alternative Scenarios:**
  - *4a. Consultation results in agreement to retain lesson:* Manager reinstates original teacher; system restores lesson status to `Confirmed` and logs resolution.
  - *5a. Zero qualified substitutes available:* Manager releases studio to vacant pool. System transitions lesson status to `Unassigned`, places student on priority rescheduling list, dispatches cancellation notice to student, and logs audit record.

# 4. Object-Oriented Analysis

## 4.1 4-Step Object-Oriented Derivation

### 4.1.1 Step 1: Solution Abstract (Domain Narrative)

The client music academy operates five physical studios (Studio 1 to Studio 5) across 86 weekly operating hours (Mon–Fri 09:00–21:00, Sat–Sun 08:00–21:00), providing individual 30-minute lessons across Piano, Drums, Violin, and Trumpet. The academy serves 251 enrolled students, staffed by nine teachers, two shift managers, and IT administrators. The web-based system replaces spreadsheet rosters with a visual decision-support platform. Human managers allocate weekly jobs assisted by sub-second clash validation. Availability must be submitted by 23:59 on the 19th of each month (planning begins on the 20th); late submissions route to manager review. Physical constraints dictate that all studios house pianos, but only Studio 5 houses an acoustic drum kit. Pedagogical safety requires that instructors teach at most 4.0 continuous hours before receiving a mandatory 1.0-hour break. Statutory rules monitor the 44-hour weekly threshold under Singapore's Employment Act 1968, tracking authorized overtime at 1.5x. All mutations, rejections, and managerial overrides are recorded in an append-only audit trail retained for 24 calendar months.

### 4.1.4 Step 4: Relationship Derivation

- **Generalization (Inheritance):** `User` (abstract) is the superclass for `Teacher`, `Manager`, and `ITAdministrator`, which inherit `staffId`, `fullName`, `email`, `role`, and `isActive`. `Teacher` specializes with `instrumentQualifications` and workload methods. `Manager` specializes with `managedShiftHours`. `ITAdministrator` specializes with `privilegeLevel`.
- **Composition:** `Schedule` composes `Lesson` (`1` to `0..*`) because lessons cannot exist independently of a schedule. `Teacher` composes `Availability` (`1` to `0..*`) and `Preference` (`1` to `0..1`). `Studio` composes `MaintenanceWindow` (`1` to `0..*`). `Lesson` composes `RejectionRequest` (`1` to `0..1`).
- **Association:** `Lesson` is associated with `Teacher` (`0..*` to `0..1`, role `assignedTo`) and `Studio` (`0..*` to `0..1`, role `conductedIn`). `User` initiates `AuditLog` records (`1` to `0..*`), which target `Schedule` and record `Lesson` mutations.
- **Dependency:** Strict Entity-Control-Boundary layering is enforced: Boundary classes depend only on Control classes (invokes). Boundary `AllocationUI` invokes `AllocationController`, which delegates rule validation to `ConflictValidator` (validates via). `SwapController` delegates to `ConflictValidator`. Boundary `ManagerDashboardUI` invokes `WorkloadController`, `ReportController`, and `ComplianceController`. Human actors never call Control or Entity classes directly.

## A.1 MIN-01-MGR: Management Operations (Marcus Tan)

**Date:** 15 September 2026 | **Duration:** 75 min | **Venue:** Music School Admin Office / MS Teams\
**Interviewee:** Marcus Tan (Operations Co-Manager)\
**Attendees:** Joseph Poon (Lead Interviewer), Ryan Ke (Minute Taker), Marcus Tan

**Objectives:** Elicit operational workflows, scheduling heuristics, and pain points for weekly allocations; establish business rules for workload caps, rest periods, and overtime thresholds; formalize late availability and job rejection exception handling.

**Key Findings:**
- **Manual Overhead:** Constructing weekly rosters in unversioned Excel spreadsheets consumes an estimated 8 to 12 hours per cycle and frequently results in undetected room double-bookings.
- **3-Tier Workload Badges:** Workload indicator requires three tiers: Green (<35.0h), Amber (35.0h–40.0h), and Red (>40.0h). The top 3 lowest-workload teachers must be displayed prominently to guide allocation equity.
- **Side-by-Side Allocation Matrix:** Manager compares up to 3 candidate teachers side-by-side, reviewing availability, running hours, preferences, and physical studio location for adjacent slots on that date.
- **Business Constraints:** Instrument qualification is a hard constraint; continuous teaching is capped at 4.0h with a mandatory 1.0h rest break; Studio 5 exclusively houses the drum kit.
- **Overtime Policy:** 40.0h threshold triggers an advisory soft warning (40.5h–44.0h tier) that managers can override with logged justification; 44.0h statutory ceiling is enforced as an unbreachable hard block under the Employment Act.
- **Exception Queues:** Late availability changes (post-19th) and teacher job rejections route to dedicated manager exception queues for review.
- **Audit Logging:** Immutable audit trail must log all allocations, overrides, and reassignments, retained for >= 24 calendar months.

- **Governance Priority Hierarchy:** Strategic priorities are ranked: (1) Statutory legal compliance, (2) Student-teacher continuity, (3) Teacher retention and workload equity, (4) Studio occupancy. Retention and work-life balance are explicitly prioritized over filling vacant rooms.

- **Statutory Labor Compliance:** Singapore's Employment Act 1968 (Part IV Section 38) governs standard weekly hours (44h normal cap), statutory overtime pay (1.5x basic hourly rate), and a 12-hour maximum daily work spread.
- **Audit Verification:** Academy requires an append-only audit trail logging all allocation transactions, late exceptions, overrides, and deactivations, retained for >= 24 calendar months.
- **Facility Servicing:** Maintenance blackout windows must be integrated into operational scheduling to prevent room booking collisions during instrument tuning.
