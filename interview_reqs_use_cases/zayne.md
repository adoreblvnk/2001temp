# Interview Questions

## Manager (4 qns)

- What is the most important feature in the app for it to replace your daily workflow?
    
- Can you describe the biggest painpoint in your current workflow pertaining to rostering teachers?
    
- How much time do you typically spend rostering your staff?
    
- How do you ensure studios are utilised efficiently?
    

## Teacher (3 qns)

- How do you currently inform management about your availability?
    
- How far in advance do you know your teaching schedule?
    
- Describe the process if you wanted to reject a job
    

## IT Admin (2 qns)

- Do you get a lot of questions from staff or managers about how to facilitate rostering?
    
- How do you manage when new staff are hired in the school?
    

## Student

- Do you typically get your first choice in timeslot?
    
- What instrument do you play? (general qn for interviewee to open up)
    

- How often do you book classes?
    
- Do you typically struggle to find a studio for your instrument?
    

## External Stakeholders

- Working hard or hardly working? (joke, don’t put this in)
    
- What do you think is the strength of this school?
